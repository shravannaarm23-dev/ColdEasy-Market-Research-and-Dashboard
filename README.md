# ColdEasy Market Research and Power BI Dashboard

## Overview
This repository contains a market research case study and a Power BI dashboard developed for the ColdEasy Technology. The analysis focuses on competitive benchmarking, customer segmentation, and strategic recommendations for low-cost cold storage solutions in rural supply chains.

## Objectives
- Evaluate the market potential for ColdEasy Technology.
- Compare ColdEasy with competing cooling solutions.
- Identify high-priority customer segments.
- Support go-to-market and business strategy decisions.

## Analysis Covered
- Price comparison
- Capacity comparison
- Technology benchmarking
- Resource requirement analysis

- Priority scoring and tier classification

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- Market Research Frameworks

## Key Insights
- ColdEasy offers significantly higher storage capacity than competing solutions.
- High-value crop farmers and FPOs emerged as the most attractive target segments.
- Competitive analysis highlighted differences in technology, pricing, and resource requirements.
- Priority scoring helped identify the most promising markets for expansion.
